| 19  | Gradient button label contrast                            | DESIGN.md sets the primary button label to #222222 on the bioluminescent gradient (teal #00827c to pale aqua #cbfffc, interpolated in sRGB). Calculated WCAG contrast (not yet verified against a live screenshot — no headless browser was available in this pass) ranges from 3.40:1 at the teal edge to 14.58:1 at the aqua edge. Padding is a fixed 22px on each side regardless of button width, so a label's horizontal center always sits at the gradient's midpoint: 7.48:1 there for every label length, which passes AA (4.5:1) and nearly AAA (7:1). Only the leading 1–2 glyphs of long, multi-word labels (e.g. "CREATE WORK ORDER", first pixel about 4.07:1) dip under 4.5:1 right at the gradient's teal edge; short labels (up to about 8 characters) clear AA everywhere. No gradient change made, per the design requirement not to change the gradient without asking.
